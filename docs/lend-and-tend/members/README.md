# Using Lend & Tend: a guide for members

Lend & Tend connects people who have garden space with people who want somewhere to
grow. One of you is a **lender** (you have the garden), the other a **tender** (you do the
gardening), and the site helps you find each other, talk, and agree how it will work.

Read the guides in order if you are new, or jump to what you need:

1. **[Getting started](getting-started.md)** - creating an account without a password,
   telling us whether you are lending or tending, the joining fee, and finding your way
   around.
2. **[Lending your garden](lending-your-garden.md)** - listing your garden, what is
   public and what stays private, photos, and editing your listing later.
3. **[Tending a garden](tending-a-garden.md)** - posting what you are looking for,
   browsing the map, and getting in touch with a lender.
4. **[Agreements and check-ins](agreements-and-check-ins.md)** - the chat, the Garden
   Sharing Agreement, what to expect over a season, and staying in touch.
5. **[Your account](your-account.md)** - your profile, email alerts, travel radius,
   membership, and getting help.

## The idea in one minute

- A **lender** lists a garden. A **tender** lists what they would like to do, or simply
  browses the map.
- Locations on the map are blurred to about a kilometre. Your exact address is only ever
  shared by you, privately, with the person you choose.
- When you find a match you **message** each other on the site, then fill in a short
  **Garden Sharing Agreement** that you both accept.
- Browsing is free. Sending and receiving messages needs a one-off **£12 joining fee**,
  with a free **concession** for anyone who needs it.
- Over the season, Lend & Tend nudges you both to **check in** so the arrangement stays
  healthy.
